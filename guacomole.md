Ingredients:
- 2 avocados
- 1 lime
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 medium red onion
- 1/2 of medium jalepeno

Instructions: 
- cut and mash avocados in a molcajete ([What is a molcajete?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molcajete))
- chop  onion and jalepeno, chop size to personal preference
- squeeze lime
- add salt
- and mix well
